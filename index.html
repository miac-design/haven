The Problem

Hotels Are Data Silos

When one hotel identifies suspicious activity, that intelligence never reaches neighboring properties. Traffickers simply relocate—and the cycle continues.

The Solution

Federated Intelligence Sharing

HAVEN uses federated learning—the same technology that powers privacy-preserving AI on your smartphone—to share pattern intelligence without sharing data.

On-Premise Analysis

Your guest data never leaves your property. HAVEN analyzes booking patterns locally, extracting only behavioral signals—not identities.

Federated Learning

Hotels share model updates (mathematical gradients), not raw data. The collective model improves without any single property seeing others' guests.

Real-Time Risk Scores

When a booking matches learned trafficking patterns across the network, your property receives an alert—even if you've never seen that pattern before.

Zero Trust Architecture

Even HAVEN's central coordinator cannot reconstruct guest data. Differential privacy guarantees mathematical protection.

Academic Foundation

Built on Rigorous Research

Federated Learning Architecture

Based on Google's Federated Averaging algorithm, enhanced with Byzantine-robust aggregation for adversarial resilience.

Differential Privacy

Mathematical privacy guarantees using ε-differential privacy, ensuring no individual booking can be reconstructed.

Hospitality Signals
